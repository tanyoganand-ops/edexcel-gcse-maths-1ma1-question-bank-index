# Specification facts (draft)

Source: Pearson Edexcel GCSE (9-1) Mathematics specification 1MA1, https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/GCSE/mathematics/2015/specification-and-sample-assesment/gcse-maths-2015-specification.pdf

| Tiers | Two tiers. Foundation grades 1 to 5. Higher grades 4 to 9 (grade 3 allowed). | 13, 33 | FETCHED |
| Papers | Three papers per tier, all from the same tier, all in the same series. Paper codes 1MA1/1F-3F and 1MA1/1H-3H. | 33-35 | FETCHED |
| Paper length and marks | Each paper 1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 33.33% of the qualification. | 33-35 | FETCHED |
| Calculator | Paper 1 no calculator. Papers 2 and 3 calculator allowed. | 13, 33-35 | FETCHED |
| Series | May/June and November, with November entry restricted (see the November resits section). First assessment May/June 2017. | 13, 33, 40 | FETCHED |
| Content strands | Number, Algebra, Ratio proportion and rates of change, Geometry and measures, Probability, Statistics. | 33 | FETCHED |
