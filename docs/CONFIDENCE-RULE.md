# Confidence values (Higher and Foundation)

Values are high, medium or low. Nothing else is used.

- high: the label was inherited from a Higher twin whose method was verified against the scheme pages, or the label was confirmed from a page image or a direct page read.
- medium: the question text and the scheme method were read in review and the label fits. For Foundation single-part questions this means the one-pass batch review (question text and scheme opening) or the fuller random/flagged reads, not a line-by-line read of every scheme.
- low: no individual review, or most of the wording was lost in extraction and no OCR or page view was done (none remain after the page-OCR pass on the 66 that had lost wording), or the per-part mark split is unknown (nov2019-1f-27).

Foundation method notes: questions without a verified Higher twin get a hand-written note in foundation-method-notes.csv, written from that question's scheme. Rows still blank have methodNoteStatus 'unfinished' in questions-foundation.json. Generated structural notes were withdrawn.
